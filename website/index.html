Questions

Good to know

Which areas do you deliver to?

We deliver across Tanzania, including mine sites and remote plants. Tell us your site and we will confirm transport and lead time in the quote.

Can you quote in US dollars?

Yes. We quote and invoice in Tanzanian shillings or US dollars, whichever suits your purchasing.

Do you supply items that are not on your product list?

Very likely. Our list shows our main ranges, but sourcing is our job. Send us the part number, brand or specification and we will find it.

Do you provide safety data sheets and certificates?

Yes. Chemicals and lubricants come with safety data sheets, and we provide certificates of conformity or origin where the manufacturer issues them.

Can we set up a regular supply contract?

Yes. We offer framework agreements with agreed prices and scheduled deliveries for consumables you use every month.

How quickly will I get a quote?

Most requests get a reply within one working day. Complex or imported items may take a little longer, and we will tell you when to expect it.
